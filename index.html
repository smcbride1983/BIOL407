Data notes and unresolved entries

EC is provisionally labeled µS/cm. Elevation is in meters; temperature is in °C. Precipitation units remain pending; VPD is in kPa. WHC is expressed as water mass divided by dry soil mass × 100. VPD values are retained as supplied; the reported conversion was division by 10. Suspect precipitation and VPD entries are shown in profiles but omitted from relationships. Caledon Shoots/Roots are excluded until conflicting labels are resolved. Blanks are missing, never zero. The repeated Holyoke Field pH/EC row was counted once; laboratory blanks are excluded. DNA is extract concentration, not microbial biomass.

New Jersey markers use representative locations, not exact sampling points. Other coordinates are those supplied for the class. Requires internet access to load the map and chart libraries.
